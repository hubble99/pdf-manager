## Description
Dokuwu is a desktop toolkit for everyday document and image work. Merge reports, pull pages out of a contract, shrink a scan before emailing it, turn a PDF into a Word file, turn photos into a PDF, or fix a typo in an existing document, all in one app.

Everything runs on your own PC. Dokuwu has no account, no cloud upload and no tracking. Your files are processed on your device, never uploaded, and nothing is kept after you close the app, which makes it a good fit for confidential documents.

Your originals stay safe: results are saved to Downloads under a new name and the source file is never touched. Edit Content, the text-correction tool, checks every change before saving and refuses an edit it cannot prove is safe, rather than risk damaging your document.

What you can do:
• Merge several PDFs into one, in the order you choose
• Split a PDF or take out the pages and ranges you need
• Organize pages visually: reorder, rotate, mirror, delete and insert pages
• Turn PDF pages into PNG, JPEG or WEBP images, or combine images into one PDF
• Turn a PDF into an editable Word document
• Convert WEBP images to PNG or JPG, many at once
• Compress PDFs and photos
• Add text, pictures, shapes, freehand drawings and highlights with Canvas PDF
• Correct existing text with Edit Content, checked before saving
• Protect PDFs with a password or remove one
• Create QR codes and barcodes (Code128, EAN13, EAN8, Code39)
• Light, dark, rose and steel-blue themes; English and Indonesian

Dokuwu is free and open source (GNU AGPL v3 or later): https://github.com/hubble99/pdf-manager

Notes:
• Edit Content works on native, selectable text. Scanned or image-only pages are view-only (no OCR).
• Requires Windows 10 version 1809 or later, 64-bit.
